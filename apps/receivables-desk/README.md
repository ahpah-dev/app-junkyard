# Receivables Desk

Track outstanding invoices, log partial payments, and see which accounts are coming due.

## Use the app

Create an invoice with its issue date, due date and face value. Track collection rate and weighted past-due days across the portfolio. Open a row to record a payment, reverse the most recently entered payment, inspect estimated 18% APR late interest, or download an account statement. Filter by aging bucket and export CSV or JSON for reconciliation.

## Data and calculations

Invoice totals and payments use USD cents. Aging uses calendar days after the due date and immediately marks fully settled invoices paid. The example is a cash-collection register with no payment processor or immutable accounting audit trail.

## Build on it

Connect an invoicing provider, keep append-only payment events on a server and add reminder schedules to turn the ledger into a collections service.
